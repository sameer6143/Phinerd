The process

From hiring brief to onboarded intern

  1. 01

    Share the brief

    Tell us the role, the skills that matter, and what "good" looks like for your team. Ten minutes, not a job-posting template.

  2. 02

    We curate, case by case

    Our team reviews applicants from partner institutions against your brief — not a keyword filter, an actual read — and builds a short, ranked list.

  3. 03

    You interview the shortlist

    Meet a handful of genuinely relevant candidates. Pick who fits. We handle scheduling and offer logistics.

  4. 04

    Cohort-led mentorship

    Your intern joins a mentor-led cohort alongside peers at other startups — structured check-ins, peer accountability, fewer dropped balls.

For startups

Built for teams too small to hire a recruiter

You don't have a talent team. You have a Wednesday and a role that needs filling. Phinerd is the curation layer . We do the sorting so your first call is with someone worth talking to.

  • Hyper-curated, not crowdsourced. Every shortlist is assembled by a human against your specific brief ,no algorithmic keyword match, no 300-applicant inbox.
  • Talent from institutions that pre-filter for you. Candidates come from a vetted network of top colleges and universities across India.
  • Mentorship built in. Interns join a structured, mentor-led cohort, so you get the output without becoming a full-time manager.
  • Priced for early-stage. No enterprise contracts. Pay for the roles you're actually filling.

For students

Don't just intern. Join a cohort.

You're matched to a startup role that actually fits your skills, then you don't go it alone. You join a small, mentor-led cohort with other Phinerd interns: structured check-ins, real feedback, and people to compare notes with.

  • Matched, not mass-applied. You apply once. We match you to roles that fit your skills and interests, no spraying your résumé across fifty listings.
  • A mentor who's actually assigned to you. Every cohort is paired with a mentor who checks in on your work, not just your attendance.
  • Peers going through it with you. Weekly cohort touchpoints with interns at other startups — so you're never figuring out your first internship in isolation.
  • Real ownership, early. Early-stage startups hand over real work fast. You'll ship things that matter, not fetch coffee.

For investors

A curation layer on top of India's talent pipeline

Generic job boards optimize for volume. Enterprise staffing firms optimize for large accounts. Phinerd sits in between: a mentor-led, cohort-based model built specifically for the startups that need talent most and can least afford to get a hire wrong.

  • Defensible by process, not just brand. The hand-curation and mentor-led cohort structure is the product, hard to replicate with a marketplace model.
  • Two-sided network, compounding. Each successful placement strengthens both the institution relationships and the startup base referring the next cohort.
  • Founder-led, in-market. Built and operated day-to-day by a founder working directly with startups and candidates, not a delegated ops layer.
